# Current-shape independent layer copies

`cloneLayers` is the explicit **current-shape independent copy** command. It evaluates the addressed source snapshot at its saved angle and saved state, gives the selected geometry, layers, paint, relations and interval ranges fresh IDs, and creates one new owned snapshot. The destination references this new root. Editing an old source, its mirror partner, parent, Warp, membership or Recorder material basis no longer changes the copy. `pasteLayers` remains a live reference to the addressed source.

The copy preserves the evaluated positions and handles, visible/hidden ink, fixed stroke widths and profiles, fill colors and boundaries, offset followers, interval modes, enabled flags and end brushes, local links/groups/joins, and relative paint order. Selected layers must include their explicit curve, paint and display-route dependencies. Excluded source members and membership tombstones are absent from the new root. Draft edits must be saved before copying a snapshot by address.

The source's already evaluated shape becomes the independent base. Its old shape residuals and Warps are not replayed. A deferred affine material source is retained only where rebuilding ARC geometry from placed control points would change the appearance. A common affine becomes a local layer domain, including shear, reflection and exact zero scale. Different native stroke affines within one layer use curve-owned placement. These operations belong to the new copy and do not refer back to its former ancestors. New edits use the normal local shape, placement, relation and material commands.

Copying evaluates and validates a detached candidate after a JSON round trip before publishing any new objects. It checks canonical geometry and appearance fields, derived ARC cubics, interval coverage and material support positions, fill/offset geometry, and paint order. The public batch and its Undo remain atomic; the input project is unchanged on failure and during dry runs.

Unsupported material is rejected with `INDEPENDENT_COPY_UNSUPPORTED` and the source snapshot and affected object IDs. Current limits include different sheared/reflected material affines inside a single layer, transient pinch material, evaluated relation values beyond the stored authoring range, translated offsets under a collapsed affine, and paint ordering that still depends on a former source context. A mismatch in the candidate's geometry, relationships or appearance also rejects the whole copy. No hidden recursive ancestor copy or lossy fallback is created.

Regression coverage includes the asymmetric mirrored-partner failure, nested shape residuals, reflected/sheared/collapsed ARC and rendered ink, fill color and width, cross-layer display ARC routes, translated offsets, materialized real-view recipes, source independence, later local editing, fresh identity mapping, JSON reload, Undo/Redo, and diagnostic failures without history changes.
