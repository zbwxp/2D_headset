# V0.12.21 · Keyboard Nudge

Drawing selection now routes through one keyboard nudge dispatcher. Arrow keys move selected segments, strokes, organizational groups, layer contents, nodes, handles, fills, offset followers and the reference image. Shift uses five times the normal step; Alt uses one fifth. Explicitly selected hidden geometry can move without becoming visible. Locked members and locked geometry affected by binding still reject the entire operation.

Pure translation of a segment propagates its shared nodes and adjacent handles through the existing relationships without asking to select whole neighbouring curves. Their other endpoints remain unchanged. Node and handle edits keep existing binding and tangent constraints. A fill moves its source boundary and stays attached. A selected offset follower can move independently via optional `OffsetRelation.translation`, applied after deriving its source offset. Old assets default to zero translation; Save/Load validates and preserves the optional field. Detaching a translated follower bakes its visible position. When a source and follower are both selected and the complete source translates, the follower follows once rather than receiving a second translation.

The mirror axis is explicitly selected by its grip/line, highlighted, and adjustable with left/right arrows or its numeric field. Display interval markers still move along the path. Ink endpoint arrows project onto the endpoint's outward tangent and change extension, preserving source geometry. Reference movement observes its existing bounds and lock.

Held keys preview one gesture and commit one Undo entry on release, pointer selection changes, window blur or workspace exit. Escape/Undo during a held preview cancels that preview. Switching selection cannot drop or double-commit pending movement. Text/number fields and menus retain their native keyboard behavior. No-op/clamped movement creates no history. Nudging during pen continuation updates the continuation anchor and its Undo/Redo snapshots.

Validation: 137 Drawing/i18n unit tests, 33 browser regressions and the production build passed. New checks cover every movement category, relation preservation, hidden/locked members, mixed fill/curve/offset selection, offset persistence and detach, held-key lifecycle, input focus, and pen continuation after a nudge. Existing drag, selection/history, group/layer and Stage 4 paint regressions pass.
