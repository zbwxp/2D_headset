## 2. What it exports (each a port of the v103 function named)
- `quadProjection(rect, quad)` → `{ map, vector, denominator, matrix, affine }` (cageField.ts `quadProjection`): normalized 8×8 DLT by Gauss–Jordan with pivoting; `vector` the analytic Jacobian; refuses a crossed or flattened quad and points past the projective horizon (den < 1e-4), with v103's messages.
- `bendPoint`, `bendVector`, `assertBend`, `inverseBend`, `neutralBend`, `isNeutralBend`, `bendEdges` (coons.ts): the displacement form of the bilinearly blended Coons patch on the unit square; edges bottom, right, top, left, two handles each; the fold guard (det ≥ 0.015 at 17×17 sample points — a sampled check, not a guarantee for the whole region) and the |n| ≤ 8 bound.
- `deformField(rect, quad, bend?)` (cageField.ts `drawingDeformProjection`): the composed field **H(Coons(p)) — the Coons bend first, then the homography H**; a neutral or disabled bend returns the exact homography; the 17×17 interior horizon check.
- **Invalid input is refused, never repaired:** a non-finite number anywhere — every public function checks its inputs and its return values (dot 1791891870), the disabled-bend pass-through included; the containers too — a bend (`enabled` a boolean, four edges of two finite points) and a parameter table (≥ 2 finite values, finite source knots of the same count) are checked by every function that takes one (dot 1791892166); a source frame with no width or height (< 1e-7, v103's threshold) — and, added beyond v103 (which lets a NaN frame through as NaN results), a non-finite source frame; a crossed or flattened quad (v103's cross-product threshold); a point whose denominator is below 1e-4 (the positive-denominator side only); a non-finite fitted result. Control points outside the frame are mapped as they are (and refused only past the horizon) — never clamped into the frame.
- `fitDeformedCubic(cubic, field)` → `{ shape, parameters, maxError }` and `fitCubicTarget` (cubicDeformation.ts): affine field → the control points mapped exactly; otherwise the v103 fit — end points exact, tangent rays from `field.vector`; the handle lengths are bounded to [1 %, 800 %] of the **Jacobian-directed handles adjusted by `parameterScale`** (va = J·h₀ / s, vb = J·h₃ · s, s = ∛(den(P₃)/den(P₀))), not of the mapped control points; a zero handle stays zero (no end tangent can be defined there, as v103). 128-sample least squares alternating with closest-point Newton steps (projective initial parameters `deformParameter`), at most 10 rounds.
- **What the numbers mean (dot 1791891353):** `maxError` is the largest distance over 257 parameter samples between the fitted cubic and the deformed source, in the input's coordinate units — not screen pixels, not a bound over the whole curve. The 17 × 17 fold check and the 17 × 17 horizon check are sampled guards, not proofs.
- `sourceParameter` / `mappedParameter` (the t-maps), kept for later (display intervals ride on t); not used by package 2.

## 3. Evidence (tests)
- **Same numbers as v103:** a test runs the v103 functions themselves (read with `git show 7205381:…` into a fixture at test-writing time, committed as a frozen copy under `deform/test/v103/` with its source SHA in a header) and this port on the same inputs — random quads, bends and cubics — and requires equal outputs (map, vector, fitted shape, maxError) within a mixed absolute / relative tolerance of 1e-9 (|a − b| ≤ 1e-9 · max(1, |a|, |b|)).
- The comparison uses a fixed seed and checks both ways: valid inputs give equal results, invalid ones are refused by both with the same message.
- Ported v103 cases (`drawing-deform`, `drawing-curved-deform`, `drawing-deform-kernel`, `drawing-curved-cage` — the maths parts only): the four corners hit exactly; an affine quad is exact; crossing and horizon refused; neutral bend = the homography; H(Coons(p)) with analytic derivatives (checked against finite differences); one cubic per source with exact end points and tangent rays, handles not point-mapped; folded bends refused; the edge midpoint factor 4/3.
- Corner cases: a degenerate rect (zero width / height) refused; a non-finite rect, quad or cubic refused; a cubic with a zero-length handle keeps it zero; a reversed cubic; a control point outside the frame is mapped, not clamped.
