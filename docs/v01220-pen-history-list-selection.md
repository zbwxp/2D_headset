# V0.12.20 · Pen History and List Selection

Drawing Room now owns Ctrl/Cmd+Z and redo shortcuts outside text fields, avoiding duplicate history handling. Undo during continuous pen drawing removes one completed segment and restores the previous anchor and continuation; Redo restores the segment and continuation, including closed strokes. The uncommitted first anchor can be cancelled without undoing an unrelated document edit. Transient continuation snapshots are keyed by document identity and never saved in the asset. Switching tools or ending the pen session discards those snapshots. Input fields keep native text editing shortcuts.

The right sidebar uses the displayed hierarchy and order for selection. Click establishes an anchor; Shift-click selects the inclusive range and subsequent Shift-clicks can expand, shrink or reverse it. Ctrl/Cmd-click toggles an item; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift-click adds a range. Closed stroke and group headers represent their complete contents, including owned fills. Open individual segment rows remain independently selectable. Canvas selection clears the list anchor. List selections do not scroll back to the first selected item or force folders open.

Delete/Backspace, the new “Delete selected” button, and the properties Delete button atomically remove selected objects. Hidden objects are selectable and deletable; locked objects and locked join partners still reject deletion. A selection can contain segments, complete strokes/groups, fills and offset followers. List Ctrl/Cmd+A includes hidden members and paint objects. Delete Layer remains a separate action. Existing topology, interval and group cleanup runs after deletion, and deleting only part of a fill boundary leaves its existing invalid-region diagnostic rather than repairing geometry.

No saved geometry schema change. Validation: 131 Drawing/i18n unit tests, 42 distinct browser regressions, and production build passed. Browser checks include per-segment keyboard and header Undo/Redo, continued pen drawing, closure, forward/reverse/shrinking list ranges, modifier selection, hidden and locked members, mixed fill/curve deletion, collapsed headers, input focus, drag handling, group/layer transfer, Stage 4 painting and existing stroke operations.
